    How the number is built

    AcquirePostings fetched from Indeed France, after a robots.txt check on the demo page.
    StructureA local LLM (qwen3:8b, Ollama) turns each description into a list of hard skills.
    CheckSpelling variants merged, soft skills and job titles removed, empty extractions counted.
    CountOne skill counts once per posting; whole-skill match, not substring.
    PublishOnly the counts below leave the laptop — never a posting.

    Limits

    • One job board, one search on one day: not a census of the French market.
    • The model can miss or invent a skill; a classmate cross-checked the raw extraction.
    • A skill being mentioned says nothing about the level required.

    What is published, and what is not

    • Published: counts per skill, the two KPIs, quality counters.
    • Never published: posting titles, company names, descriptions, per-posting skill lists — they stay in data/, which is git-ignored.
    • Indeed's terms forbid redistributing scraped postings; aggregates do not reproduce them.
